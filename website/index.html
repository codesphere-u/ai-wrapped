Questions users ask first

How does AI Wrapped avoid leaking secrets into top keywords?

All 11 patterns in SECRET_REDACT_PATTERNS (covering OpenAI/Anthropic sk- keys, GitHub/HF/Groq tokens, Google AIza keys, AWS keys, JWTs, Bearer headers, emails, SSNs, and cards) are replaced before frequency counting or rendering HTML/Markdown.

Does it work on newer sharded ChatGPT ZIP exports?

Yes. It parses both classic conversations.json and multi-shard conversations-000.json exports, resolving active current_node branches while ignoring internal recipient != all tool payloads.

How do I switch between UTC and my local wall clock?

Pass --tz local to adjust the 24-hour circadian chart, 2–5 AM rabbit-hole metric, and peak activity hour to your machine timezone.
